Changing the sketch does nothing (+0.9 pts, p = 0.70). Changing the caption does nothing (+2.4 pts, p = 0.31). On the same scenes the caption moves π0.5 by +13.7 pts (p < 0.001). The checkpoint is a LoRA fine-tune of π0.5-LIBERO, so it had that sensitivity before training and lost it.

3. The same split, as one picture

Of the grasps each arm completes, the share landing on the designated object, split by whether that object is the default instance. Indifference to the conditioning predicts chance (~30%). Zero out of 437 is below chance — a policy cannot land below chance by ignoring its inputs. It lands there by reaching a fixed position.

4. Where each rollout dies

Every rollout falls in exactly one bucket, so each bar sums to 100%.

Two separate failures, not one. The checkpoint never grasps anything in 47% of rollouts; π0.5 does that 1% of the time — that is motor competence, before any question of which object. And of the grasps it does make, the caption and the sketch make no difference to what it picks. For π0.5 the caption does move it: wrong-object rate is 39% under explicit captions against 52% under ambiguous ones.
